# Code of Conduct

This code applies to everyone participating in the LEVANTE core-tasks project, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions about this repository.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep feedback about the work, and make it specific enough for someone to act on.
- Give contributors time. Reviews of outside pull requests can take a while, as described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](.github/CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Protect participants. This software is used to collect data from children, caregivers, and teachers. Do not post, commit, or attach participant data, recordings, screenshots of real sessions, or anything that identifies a participant.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, harassment, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or exclusion based on identity, background, or beliefs.
- Sexual attention or comments that are not appropriate for a professional project.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Disrupting discussion, reviews, or issues so that other people cannot participate.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to [levante-support@stanford.edu](mailto:levante-support@stanford.edu). Do not put the report in a public issue or pull request.

Maintainers will look into the report, keep it confidential to the extent they can, and respond with what they decide to do. That can include a warning, removing comments, or blocking further participation.

## Questions

Questions about how to contribute belong in a GitHub issue or pull request, following [CONTRIBUTING.md](.github/CONTRIBUTING.md).
